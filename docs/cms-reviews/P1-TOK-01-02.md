## Fix round 1

The review of `a8dbaef` found two blocking problems and ten smaller ones. I checked each against the code before changing anything.

### Blocking 1: a store that had its own colours could not publish the platform's own look

**The review was right.** Reproduced before any change, with the review's own cases (`checkBrand` on `a8dbaef`'s `lib/brand.js`):

- Reset (brand `null`, Pulse kit) against the seeded teal brand live: `ok:false`, three errors, no suggestion: "Secondary text on grey chips: #71717b on #f4f4f5 has a contrast of 4.39 to 1 … (7 more colour pairs …)", "Footer wordmark accent: #65a30d on #fafafa … 2.96 to 1 …", "Error messages: #e7000b on #fef2f2 … 4.36 to 1 …".
- The Pulse kit's brand (`#84cc16` / `#ffffff` / `#0a0a0b`) and white / `#0a0a0b` with a blue accent, against the same live brand: `ok:false`, with "Try text colour #080808" and "Try background colour #fcfcfc".

**Cause** (as the review said): a brand with the platform's white and `#0a0a0b`, and no brand at all, keep the default greys and status colours (`neutralDerived` is false). The default look has 14 failing pairs. `checkBrand` judged them only against the live store, so whenever the live store showed derived (readable) colours, each of the 14 counted as a change and blocked. The packet's known limit ("only ever warns at publish") was false.

**The fix** (`lib/brand.js` `pairVerdicts`, used by both `checkBrand` and `publishBlocked`): a failing pair whose two colours are exactly the platform default look's pair on the same theme kit is the platform's own (**inherited**). It never blocks, and it is not reported to the founder (no banner, no message, no suggestion); it stays in `checkBrand().failing` and `.inherited` for the record. Only pairs a founder colour moves are excused this way; a theme kit's own surfaces (`fix: null`: header and hero text) are still judged against the live store. A brand that moves any grey (for example `#757575` text) is judged as before.

This also answers two of the smaller findings: the odd "Try text colour #080808" / "Try background colour #fcfcfc" suggestions and the permanent "Hard to read" banner for every store with white and `#0a0a0b` came from these same 14 pairs, and are gone.

**Tests** (`test:tokens`): "REGRESSION: over a store that had its own colours, a reset, the Pulse kit's brand and white/#0a0a0b with a new accent all publish" (pure, plus `publishBlocked`); "REGRESSION, real routes: with a theme pinned and the store's own colours live, Reset, the Pulse kit with its brand, and white/#0a0a0b with a new accent each publish" (the real Brand route, the real theme route, the real publish, 200 each; the Reset answer has no warning); and the control "the platform's own pairs excuse only identical colours: a brand that moves a grey still blocks". On `a8dbaef`'s `lib/brand.js` the first two fail (`drills/TOK-01-02/fix1/test-tokens-on-a8dbaef-brand.txt`: 45 passed, 5 failed).

**Browser** (production build, real API as the signed-in owner, `results-fix1.json`): A. seeded brand live on a pinned Pulse kit, then Reset: publishes (200), live brand `null`. B. seeded brand live again, then the Pulse kit applied with its brand: publishes (200), and the Brand screen shows no "Hard to read" banner ([screenshot](drills/TOK-01-02/fix1-brand-pulse-kit-brand.png)). No publish attempt answered 422.

### Blocking 2: a store with no brand could not publish after applying the Glow kit the default way

**The review was right.** Reproduced: `checkBrand(null, { kit: "glow-1" })` on `a8dbaef` gave `ok:false`, "Header wordmark accent: #a3e635 on #fffbf7 has a contrast of 1.46 to 1, and large text needs at least 3 to 1 (1 more colour pair …)" with no suggestion, because `brandRoles` corrected header and hero accents only when a brand existed, the suggestion was skipped for a `null` brand, and the Brand page computed nothing without a saved brand.

**The fix:**

- `brandRoles(null, kit)`: an accent role whose every enforced pair sits on a kit surface (`header-accent`, `hero-accent`) is corrected against that kit, as for any brand. On Glow: header wordmark `#a3e635` (1.46:1) → `#4d7c0f`, hero highlight and badge → `#3f6212`. On Pulse nothing moves (the lime already reads on the dark header), so the default look is unchanged.

- A store with no brand is suggested a change to the platform's colours (`DEFAULT_BRAND`) if anything of its own still fails. With the inherited rule above, a store with no brand can now only be refused for a kit's own surfaces.
- The Brand page checks a store with no brand too, and the Reset answer carries the same check, so the screen explains the corrected shade: "The accent in your header wordmark would be #a3e635, too light to read in the header, so your store uses #4d7c0f, a darker shade of the accent." ("the accent", not "your accent", when no brand is saved.)

**Tests:** "NO BRAND on the Glow kit: header and hero accents are corrected against the kit, the page gets them, nothing blocks" (pure: both pairs pass, `checkBrand` ok, `publishBlocked` false, the note, the CSS, and `""` on Pulse) and "NO BRAND, real routes: a store with no brand and no theme applies the Glow kit the console's default way and publishes" (Reset, theme cleared, publish 200; apply Glow with `withBrand:false, withPresets:false`, still no brand, publish 200; the Reset answer's note names the wordmark). **Browser** (`results-fix1.json`, C): no brand and no theme live (200), then Glow applied brand unticked: publishes (200). ## What was built

### Step 0: the baseline and the pixel comparison (`7f6ca66`)

- `scripts/pixel-diff.js` (new): compares two folders of PNGs pixel by pixel with `sharp` (already a dependency; no new one). For each pair: the number of differing pixels (any channel off by more than `--tolerance`, default 0), the bounding box, a size change, and with `--out` a diff image (changed pixels in red over the faded page). `node scripts/pixel-diff.js <before> <after> [--out dir] [--json file]`. Reusable for any later item.
- `scripts/drills/p1/tok.mjs` (new journey for `scripts/drills/p1/run.mjs`): captures the ten pages the brief names (home, a product, a collection, `/collections`, search, the cart drawer, checkout's first step, account sign-in, a CMS page, the 404) at 375, 768 and 1440, as lossless full-page captures, for two sets: the harness's **seeded brand** (teal accent, warm off-white background, radius xl, type large) and **no brand config** (the platform default look). The live brand is switched by publishing a copy of the live release into the local harness database and pointed back afterwards. A fixed experiment cookie and an answered cookie banner make every capture repeatable.
- The baseline was captured on the production build of `8968db3`, before any style change, and committed: small visual copies (`docs/cms-reviews/drills/TOK-01-02/baseline/`, 60 PNGs, 912KB) and a manifest with the SHA-256 of every lossless capture (`baseline-manifest.json`). The lossless captures stay outside the repo; the comparison runs on them.
- **Noise floor**, measured before any change: two more captures of the same build against the baseline: 118 of 120 shots byte-identical; two differ by 1 pixel (one channel off by 1) and 8 pixels (a 1×8 anti-aliased strip). `compare/noise-*.json`.

### TOK-01: the storefront uses semantic colour roles (`0bb240c`)

- **The roles** (`lib/brand.js` `ROLES`, CSS variables `--sf-<role>` in `app/globals.css`, Tailwind colours `--color-sf-<role>`, so components write `bg-sf-surface`, `text-sf-text-muted`, `border-sf-border`). The ten the spec names are there: surface (`surface`), muted surface (`surface-muted`), text (`text`), muted text (`text-muted`), border (`border`), accent (`accent`), text on accent (`on-accent`), sale (`sale`, `on-sale`, `sale-soft`, `on-sale-soft`), success (`success`…), danger (`danger`…), plus warning, the header and hero accents (`header-accent`, `hero-accent`, `hero-glow`; the header and hero surfaces stay the kit's `--header-*` / `--hero-*`), inverse (solid neutral buttons and selected chips), marks (arrows, ×, separators), accent text and tints, photo overlays and media placeholders.
- **Steps inside a role.** Today's look uses seven greys for text (zinc-900 down to zinc-300). To keep the default look pixel-identical, each grey got its own step: `text`, `text-soft`, `text-body`, `text-muted`, `text-subtle`, `text-faint`, `mark`; surfaces `surface`, `-muted`, `-sunken`, `-strong`, `-disabled`; borders `border-soft`, `border`, `border-strong`, `border-hover`. 63 roles in all (the table, with what each replaced, is `ROLES`).
- **Defaults are exactly what the classes rendered.** Each default is Tailwind v4's own value (the oklch in `tailwindcss/theme.css`, which the build compiles to the same `lab()` it uses for the class), accent roles alias today's ladder step (`--sf-accent-text: var(--accent-700)`). A test checks every default against Tailwind.
- **A brand with its own colours derives every role from them** (`brandRoles`): text is the brand's text colour, the greys are mixes of its background and text placed where today's zinc steps sit, then moved just far enough that every pair the role is used in reads (the floors come from the TOK-02 pair list, so the two can't disagree). Accent roles keep their ladder step unless it reads badly on what it sits on, then move to the nearest step that reads. Status colours keep their hue. On a dark background, tints are mixed into the background. A store with no brand config gets no override on the Pulse kit; on another kit it gets only the header and hero accent corrections that kit needs (fix round 1).
- **Radius roles** (`rounded-sf-card`, `-control`, `-small`, `-xs`, `-2xs`) are multiples of the brand radius that give exactly `rounded-2xl`, `-xl`, `-lg`, `-md` and `rounded` at the default md. `rounded-full` (pills, dots) stays. **Literal sizes**: `text-[11px]` and `text-[10px]` became `text-sf-2xs` / `text-sf-3xs`, `rounded-[var(--btn-radius,…)]` became `rounded-sf-button`, `py-[var(--section-pad-y,…)]` became `py-sf-section`, and the two shadows with a colour in them became `shadow-sf-bar` / `shadow-sf-sheet`.
- **The sweep.** About 850 class tokens in 47 storefront files, cart and checkout included (class names only in `CheckoutFlow.js`; the payment-verify code is untouched). A codemod did the unambiguous ones; the ones whose role depends on where they sit (white text on a dark button vs on a photo, `zinc-300` as a mark vs as a count on a selected chip, accent-400 in the hero vs the header, savings chips as sale) were edited by hand.
- **Rich text** (`.rte` in `globals.css`): body, headings, strong, table borders and image corners use roles. Links use the accent text role; they were `#4d7c0f` (lime) on every store.
- Two fixes that fell out: the header's cart icon was `text-white` on every kit (white on the Glow kit's cream header); it follows the header text now. `layout.js` passes the theme kit to `brandCssVars`, so header and hero accents are checked against the kit's own surfaces (one line in `chromeData`, nowhere near the maintenance curtain or `generateMetadata`).
- **`scripts/test-tokens.js`** (`test:tokens`, new): see "Tests" below.
- `scripts/test-copy.js`: two selectors found elements by `bg-red-50` and `rounded bg-zinc-100`; they now use the role classes. No assertion changed.

### TOK-02: a full pairing check (`8e7b5d2`)

- **The matrix** (`lib/brand.js` `PAIRS`): 63 foreground-on-background role pairs the storefront draws (57 enforced, 6 decorative or disabled), each with a kind and the colour a founder changes to fix it: body text, headings with no colour of their own, every grey on every surface it sits on, symbols and placeholders, solid buttons (resting and hover), accent text and links, accent tints and chips, sale badge and savings chip, success, warning and error messages, the header (text, links, wordmark accent) and the hero (title, subtitle, highlight and badge, button), collection names on their cards, and the decorative and disabled ones (listed and measured, not enforced). Kinds: text 4.5:1; large text (24px+, or 18.66px+ bold) 3:1; non-text marks 3:1; decorative and disabled: none. Symbol glyphs typed as text (× → +) and placeholders count as text.
- `pairMatrix(brand, kit)` measures every pair for a brand on a theme kit (`KIT_SURFACES`, a JS mirror of the `[data-kit]` CSS, kept in step by a test). `checkBrand(brand, { kit, live, liveKit })` compares with what the live store shows: a failing pair whose colours differ from the live store's is **blocking**; one the live store already shows is a **warning**; one whose colours are exactly the platform default look's on that kit is the platform's own and neither blocks nor shows (fix round 1). Failing pairs with the same cause are grouped into one plain message that names the first one a shopper meets, its two colours and contrast, what it needs, how many more pairs have the same problem, and the **nearest colour that passes** (same hue, lightness moved the least, without breaking another pair): for example "Product titles, prices and body text: #18181b on #0a0a0b has a contrast of 1.12 to 1, and small text needs at least 4.5 to 1 (26 more colour pairs on your store have the same problem). Try text colour #80808e."
- **At save** (`PUT /api/admin/brand` and its preview call): only a malformed value is refused (422). Readability never blocks: the answer carries `warnings` (the messages above) and `notes` (where the store uses a readable shade of the accent instead, for example "The hero highlight and badge would be #454545, too dark to read on the hero, so your store uses #818181, a lighter shade of your accent."). The Brand screen shows them as you type, after Save, and on arrival (`lib/admin/brand-readability.js`). The screen's description and the Text hint no longer say "WCAG AA" or "blocks save".
- **At publish** (`validateRelease`): every failing pair becomes a `BRAND_PAIR` issue on `doc_design` (error if changed, warning if already live). The Publishing screen lists it under "Fix before publishing" ("Brand: …") and Publish now stays disabled; the publish call answers 422.
- **Before a store's first publish** the drafts are the live store, so a saved brand that the publish check would refuse is not shown to shoppers (`resolvedBrand` in `lib/content/resolve.js`, using `publishBlocked`, a cheap memoised form of the check). The founder's own preview still shows it.
- **The platform's own pairs (fix round 1).** A failing pair whose colours equal the platform default look's on the same kit never blocks and is not shown, whatever is live. So a store that resets from its own readable colours goes back to the default look's faint grey (2.6:1) without a warning. Whether to show it is an owner decision (MANUAL-CHECKS).
- **Corrections, not refusals, for derived shades.** A brand's accent shades are the system's choice, so an unreadable one is moved (the black accent's hero highlight). The founder's own three colours (accent, background, text) are never changed; a failing pair that only they can fix is refused at publish.
- Derivation refinements that came with the matrix: hover fills keep the label on them readable (accent and solid buttons); symbol glyphs and placeholders are held at 4.5:1; derived roles aim 0.05 above each line so a browser rounding step can't land under it; any brand (even one with the Pulse kit's own colours) moves an unreadable accent shade. Placeholders follow a brand through `--sf-placeholder`, unset by default so Tailwind's own placeholder colour applies to stores with no brand.
- Tests that asserted the old single check were updated to the new contract: `test-content.js` ("unreadable body text SAVES with a warning, and the matrix refuses it") and `test-releases.js` (the issue code is `BRAND_PAIR`; `BRAND_INVALID` is now only a malformed value).

## Deviations

- **More than ten roles.** The spec names ten; the look today uses seven text greys, five surfaces and four borders. Collapsing them would have changed the default look, which the spec forbids, so each became a step inside its role (`text-soft`, `text-subtle`, …). The pairing matrix covers every step.
- **Accent ladder classes were replaced too** (`text-accent-700` → `text-sf-accent-text`, …). They already followed the brand, but a fixed step can't be readable on both a light and a dark store. The lint flags raw ladder steps in storefront code.
- **Radius roles follow the brand radius**, which is part of TOK-08's scope ("Radius must reach cards, inputs, images"). At the default md radius nothing moves; the seeded brand (radius xl) gets rounder cards and fields. Button shape still doesn't reach Add to cart (TOK-08).
- **"Refused or corrected": corrected** for accent shades (the system picks them), refused for the founder's own colours.
- **"Only for changed tokens" is judged by the colours the shopper sees**, pair by pair, not by which setting was edited. A kit change that moves a pair counts as a change too.
- **Pre-first-publish guard** (beyond the brief): needed because a save no longer refuses unreadable colours, and drafts are the live store until the first publish.
- **The Pulse kit's own brand now gets accent corrections.** Its lime links were 4.15:1 (`accentShades` gives a lighter 700 than the platform's built-in lime); they become `#456c09`. A store with no brand config is untouched.
- **The platform default look keeps its own failing pairs** (faint text 2.6:1, placeholders about 3.3:1, footer and checkout wordmark accents, red text on its tint 4.36:1). The brief requires the default look unchanged; this is an owner decision (MANUAL-CHECKS).
- **The pixel comparison uses `sharp` in node**, not a canvas in Chrome. Both need no new dependency; `sharp` was already used by the drill runner.
- **The pinned comparison** first injected a second stylesheet after load (1-pixel differences: the build compiles the oklch defaults to `lab()`, and stale anti-aliased edges after repaint), then edited the HTML (broke React's length-prefixed payload). The method that held: a stylesheet added before first paint that re-declares each role with the exact value the build compiled. The control run proves it changes nothing on the default look.
- **Committed screenshots are small copies** (at most 360 wide, the top 2,000px), a visual record only; the comparisons ran on lossless captures kept outside the repo, with their SHA-256 in `baseline-manifest.json`.
- **The checkout fix hides the step arrows below 640px wide**; the numbers already give the order.
- **The default look changed in two places outside the baseline pages** (the earlier claim "each default is Tailwind's own value for the class it replaced" was not true for these): the wishlist's sold-out label was `text-amber-600` and is now the warning role (amber-700, `#bb4d00`); the order page's "cancelled" fulfilment label was `text-red-500` and is now the danger role (red-600, `#e7000b`). Both are darker (on white: amber-700 5.03:1, red-600 4.77:1); the roles fold two near shades into one. `git diff 8968db3` shows no other such change.
- **A store with no brand on the Glow kit changed** (fix round 1): its header wordmark and hero highlight are `#4d7c0f` / `#3f6212` instead of the lime 400 (1.46:1 on Glow's cream). On the Pulse kit a store with no brand is unchanged.
- **The Brand screen's copy changed** ("Anything hard to read is flagged here, and publishing waits until it's fixed"), and it shows warnings on arrival.

## Known limits

- **Desktop Chrome only, headless, on macOS, on the harness.** Not Safari, not a phone, not staging, not production. The production build ran locally (`next start`), not on Vercel's edge runtime.
- **The exit check covers home, product, collection, cart and checkout's first step** at 375 and 1440 in two themes. Account, orders, wishlist, quiz, bundles, the order confirmation, CMS pages, checkout's review and payment steps, and hover and focus states were not measured in the browser (the matrix covers their pairs by maths only).
- **The harness has no product photos.** Text over photos (collection names, sold-out badges) was measured only on the dark placeholder; on a real photo it relies on the fixed scrim.
- **Field outlines are decorative in the matrix.** Accessibility guidance asks 3:1 for a field's outline; every store is about 1.3:1 today (owner decision).
- **`text-foreground/75`** (the image-with-text section's paragraph) is a brand token with opacity and is not in the matrix; it wasn't on any page measured.
- **The platform default look keeps its failing pairs** (see Deviations). Since fix round 1 they never block and are not shown, for a store with no brand and for a brand with the platform's white and `#0a0a0b`, whatever is live. (Before fix round 1 this line said they only ever warn; that was false: they blocked whenever the live store showed readable derived colours.)
- **Corrections pick from the accent's ladder.** A brand whose ladder has no readable step falls back to mixing toward black or white, which can look unlike the accent.
- **The suggestion is the nearest passing lightness on the same hue**, which for dark text on black is a mid grey (`#80808e`), readable but not what a designer would pick.
- **Pre-first-publish guard**: a store that never published and saved a failing brand shows its shoppers the platform default look, silently. The Brand screen's warning is the only sign.
