## What and why

A store on the new editor (documents mode) could still meet the old console: old addresses, old setup links, and console readers that read the old content tables. Those tables are frozen once a store moves, so a reader of them shows content from before the move, and an old screen offers saves that are refused (ED-05, spec 8.13). This item sends every old address to its editor destination, moves the readers onto the published documents, points the setup, diff and help links at the editor, keeps the old saved page templates usable (read-only, with "Make a page from this"), and applies the D2 ruling: a banner is no longer offered on page, campaign and journal documents, because those routes don't show it.

## Design

### (1) Old addresses (`lib/admin/editor-entry.js`, the old pages)

- `LEGACY_ROUTE_DOCS` gains `/admin/storefront/designer/product` → `doc_product_layout`, `/admin/storefront/designer/collection` → `doc_collection_layout` and `/admin/upsells` → `doc_cart`. `LEGACY_ROUTE_HREFS`: `/admin/catalog` → the editor's Products list, `/admin/releases` → itself (the documents "Recently published" page, P3-EDITOR PUB-04).
- New helpers (pure, edge-safe): `catalogHref(query)` (`?product=` / `?collection=` → `entityEditorHref`, else the Products list), `upsellsHref(query)` (`?product=` → that product, where its pairs live; else `doc_cart`), `legacyPageHref(slug, documents)` (async: the page's backfill id, or an editor-made page, campaign or post found by type and slug; a missing one goes to its backfill id, which the editor opens as home with `NOTES.missingDoc`; `new` opens the editor), `editorDestination(href)` (an old console link → its editor destination), `editorPathHref(path)`, and the destinations `EDITOR_LISTS` / `PRODUCTS_LIST_HREF` (`/admin/editor?list=products`), `PAGES_LIST_HREF` (`?list=pages`) and `EDITOR_PUBLISH_HREF` (`?doc=doc_home`).
- Each old page reads the mode fresh, as the P3-ROUTES screens do (`getCmsMode`, a read error keeps today's screen, whose writes still refuse on their own read), and on a documents store redirects before reading any data: upsells, designer/product, designer/collection and pages/[slug].
- **`/admin/catalog` (deviation, see Known limits):** `?product=` and `?collection=` redirect to that entity in the editor. The bare page is **not** redirected, because Bundles and Quiz live only there (until ED-06): on a documents store it shows a link to the editor's Products list in place of the old product editor (whose saves refuse there), then the Bundles and Quiz panels.
- `GET /api/admin/catalog` keeps answering on documents stores (see (2)). Its local `shopifyAdminBase` is replaced by the import from `lib/admin/shopify-admin-url.js` (P4-LIVEROW).

### (2) Frozen-table readers (spec 8.13)

- `lib/cms/view.js` gains `publishedSource()` and `publishedGetters()`: the live release with no preview (a founder's preview cookie never lays drafts over it) as a source for the storefront's own getters (`contentGettersFor` / `candidateSource` from P4-SEAM). The live v4 list, a live legacy release as it is, or the closed store; a broken live release throws `ContentUnavailableError`. `publishedGetters()` also says `closed`. `resolve.js` is imported only on the documents path.
- `storefrontView()` on a documents store is the published documents (`documents: true`, no "customized", no revisions; the old editors redirect, their writes refuse). Legacy is unchanged.
- `lib/ai/insights.js`: the hero fact is the published home document's hero on a documents store; none on a closed store or a home with no hero; the hero block on legacy.
- `lib/admin/onboarding.js`: "Publish legal pages" reads the published page documents (type `page`) on a documents store; legacy reads `listContentPages()` as before.
- `app/api/admin/catalog/route.js`: on a documents store a product's template, modules and badge and each collection's pins are the published ones (`resolvedSurfaceTemplateFor`, `resolvedProductOverride`, `resolvedCollectionPins` over the live release: the live rows and the layout documents), and `overrides` is empty. Legacy and migrating answer as before.
- The READ guard in `scripts/test-legacy-routes.js` (below).

### (3) Setup, diff and help links

- Onboarding on a documents store: "Publish legal pages" → `/admin/editor?list=pages`, "Review your draft preview" → `/admin/editor`, "Launch" → `/admin/editor?doc=doc_home` (Publish lives there), with plain hints. "Pick a theme & brand" already went to `doc_design` (P3-DESIGN). Legacy links and hints are unchanged.
- `ReleasesAdmin` takes `cmsMode`; with `documents` each diff chip goes through `editorDestination`. `PreviewFrame` takes `cmsMode`; with `documents` it is a card linking to the editor at the same page (`/admin/editor?path=<to>`), since the old preview session is refused there. Neither is shown to a documents store today (the Publishing page shows ReleasesAdmin only on legacy, PreviewFrame lives in old editors that redirect), so these are belts; without the prop both render exactly as before.

### (4) Saved templates (DOC-09 partly pulled in)

- `app/(console)/admin/(authed)/editor/templates/page.js` (new): on a documents store it reads `cms_templates` (read-only) and lists the templates by family. Legacy and migrating show a note.
- `components/admin/SavedTemplates.js` (new, client): "Make a page from this" builds the body at the click with `pageBodyFromTemplate` and creates a draft through the documents API's `createDocument` (its own sign-in, access and mode checks), then opens it in the editor.
- `lib/admin/saved-templates.js` (new, pure): `pageBodyFromTemplate(template)` → `{ type, body, notes }`. The template's family decides the type (page, campaign or journal). Old section names go through the registry adapter. Every section and block gets a fresh id at the call. A kind the editor has no section for (or one the family doesn't take), a banner (D2), and a section the validator can't read become a text section holding its words, with a plain note shown after the click. The page takes the template's name as its title and has no web address yet, so it never clashes with a page. The server validates the body again.
- The table is never edited or deleted.

### (5) D2 ruling

- `lib/registry/sections.js`: `NOT_OFFERED` (`banner` on `page`, `campaign`, `journal`), `isOffered(kind, type)`, `NOT_SHOWN_MESSAGE` ("Banners don't show on this kind of page yet.") and `notShownNotes(body)`. The banner stays in its `documentTypes`, so a banner made before still loads, saves and publishes.
- `components/admin/editor/tree/treeModel.js`: the add list leaves it out, and add and copy refuse it there (`KIND_NOT_OFFERED`, "Banners don't show on this kind of page yet. Add a text section instead."). Home and the two layouts still offer it.
- The inherited note: on save (`lib/documents/service.js` `notesOf`, as a warning beside the product module notes) and at publish (`lib/documents/checks.js` `referenceChecks`, level `inherited`, with Remove). It never blocks.
- **The routes, checked:** all three (`/policies/<slug>`, `/campaigns/<slug>`, `/journal/<slug>`) render through `resolvedContentPage` → `pageFromBody` → `pageSectionsFromBody`, which keeps only text, rich text, image and FAQ. So the campaign and journal routes drop the banner exactly as the page route does, and the note is added for all three types. The suite renders each route over a published document with a banner to prove it.

### (6) In-batch refusals (P4-GUARD review)

The catalog POST, the upsells POST and the upsells `[id]` PUT and DELETE answer an `expose` refusal (the mode guard inside the write's batch) with its own 4xx status, code and plain words before their catch-all, so an in-batch refusal is 409, never 422 or 500. DELETE had no catch; it now has the same three steps (refusal, database failure, then a plain 500).

## Known limits

- **`/admin/catalog` is not redirected when bare (deviation from the brief).** Bundles and Quiz are managed only on that page; a redirect would remove them for every documents store (a regression). It redirects `?product=` and `?collection=`, and the bare page shows the Products list link and the two panels instead of the old product editor. The sidebar's Catalog link and Offers' "Change it in Catalog" stay correct. When ED-06 gives Bundles and Quiz their own screens, the bare page can redirect too. Owner decision below.
- **The editor's lists.** `/admin/editor?list=products` and `?list=pages` name the switcher's sections, but `app/(console)/admin/(authed)/editor/page.js` and the switcher are P4-EDITOR's: until they read `list`, these open the editor at home with its switcher (which lists products and pages). Never a dead end.
- **The saved templates page has no link in the editor yet.** It is at `/admin/editor/templates`. A link from the editor's pages section belongs to P4-EDITOR's files (or a later item).
- **Files beyond the plan's list** (the orchestrator should add them to this item): `components/admin/editor/tree/treeModel.js` (stop offering the banner; no other owner in phase 4), `lib/documents/service.js` (one line: the save-time note beside the product module notes), `app/api/admin/upsells/[id]/route.js` (the brief's PUT and DELETE live there), new `lib/admin/saved-templates.js` and `components/admin/SavedTemplates.js`, and the test updates in `scripts/test-editor-tree.js`, `scripts/test-registry-contract.js` and `scripts/test-publish-checks.js`.
- **The agency kit export** (`lib/platform/agency.js`, spec 8.13) still reads the old hero, announcement and home sections on a documents store. It is not this item's file; it is in the read guard's allowlist marked as a known limit.
- **Page routes drop more than banners.** `pageSectionsFromBody` also drops the collections grid, newest products and product rail on page, campaign and journal routes, and the editor still offers them there. D2 named only the banner; the others are the same gap (DOC-06, P1).
- **The note is "inherited" even on a page never published** (a banner can only get there from before the ruling, a template, or a hand-made API body). It is a warning and never blocks.
- **Page chips in the old diff** (`editorDestination`) keep `/admin/storefront/pages/<slug>`: a link has no store documents to find the page's id; that route redirects to the page's document.
- **One more read on two console paths:** `GET /api/admin/catalog` and the onboarding checklist (built by the console layout) now read the mode (one statement). A mode that can't be read makes the catalog GET a 5xx; onboarding then keeps today's links and reads.
- **Upsells DELETE:** an unknown failure now answers a plain 500 ("Something went wrong on our side, so nothing was deleted.") instead of the framework's 500.
- **The read guard** is a source scan of `app/`, `lib/` and `components/` (comments at line start and block comments are ignored). The "behind a mode check" entries are checked by a pattern per file, not by flow analysis.
- **Test strategy:** the rendered page tests stub each page's data (as `test-editor-routes` does) and compare with the `b6110fe` file rendered with the same stubs; the reader, route, template and D2 tests run the real modules on file databases.
