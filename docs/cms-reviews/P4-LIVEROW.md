## What and why

Until now a product's content was a draft that could never publish (`PUBLISH_NOT_AVAILABLE`), and collections had no document at all. OVL-01/02 need the founder's product edits (name, description, images, brand and type shown, badge, modules, pairs, layout) to go live without ever touching Shopify, and ED-12 needs the same for a collection's pinned products and layout. They go live as one row per product in `product_content_live` (and per collection in `collection_content_live`, migration 0024), never into the release list, so shoppers' reads can join them cheaply (EFFECTIVE, OVL07) and search can be told which products changed (`search_outbox`, OVL-17, drained by P4-SEARCH).

## Design

### (1) Registry (`lib/registry/document-types.js`, `settings.js`, `validate.js`)

- **product_content** is publishable, with settings `title`, `description_html`, `gallery`, `display_vendor`, `display_type` (overlays), `badge` (text, up to 30), `template` (`classic` | `editorial` | null), `pairs` and `keep_variant_images`, plus its section list (the modules). Defaults are all null: an empty document follows Shopify.
- **collection_content** (new type) is publishable: `pinned` (ordered product ids, up to 24) and `template` (`standard` | `sidebar` | null).
- New setting kinds: `overlay` (`{value, base_hash, base_value}` or null, the value checked as its declared `of` kind; an empty name is `TITLE_EMPTY`, structure), `gallery` (ordered `[{media, alt}]`, media refs for MED-03), `pairs` (`{title, products:[ids]}`) and `product_ids`.
- **OVL-13 allowlist, a P1 pulled forward:** gallery images may only be `https://cdn.shopify.com/…` or `/api/media/<id>` (`galleryMediaAllowed`, `GALLERY_HOSTS`). A draft holds another host with a warning; publish refuses it (`IMAGE_HOST_NOT_ALLOWED`, a type rule that is a warning in draft mode and an error at publish).
- **Shopify-owned fields:** on product content a key named `price`, `compare_at_price`, `variants`, `inventory`, `availability`/`available`, `handle`, `tags`, `product_type`, `vendor` or `options` (in settings, meta or the body) is `SHOPIFY_OWNED` in plain words ("Shopify owns the price, so it can't be changed here. …"). The service answers it as 422 `SHOPIFY_OWNED` with every issue.
- **Modules:** `PRODUCT_MODULE_KINDS = rich_text, text, faq` and `offeredKinds(type, allowed)` say what a founder can add; `image_with_text` stays registered for product content, so existing ones load and save. `productModuleNotes(body)` gives the inherited warnings (`MODULE_NOT_OFFERED`, more than 6 modules `MODULES_OVER_LIMIT`, a module over today's 80-character title or 10,000-character text `MODULE_OVER_LIMIT`). They are warnings only: nothing is ever cut. They are kept out of the type's `rules()` so the registry contract (a new section of each kind is clean) still holds; the service and publish add them to the issues they answer.
- `PUBLISH_NOT_AVAILABLE` stays in `validate.js` for a type declared `publishable: false` (none today).

### (2) Service (`lib/documents/service.js`)

- `createDocument` gives these types `await productDocumentId` / `collectionDocumentId` and the product's or collection's Shopify name as their label (kept on every save; never an id).
- **Overlay rules (OVL-04, R4), enforced here:** `normalizeOverlays(type, body, prior, raw)` runs on every save (and create) of product content:
  - null, or `{value: null}`, stores null (Reset, Use Shopify's: follow Shopify again, never a copy of `base_value`);
  - a value equal to what Shopify sends now (image addresses compared without their query string, a missing alt equal to an empty one) stores null;
  - a new value takes `base_value` and `base_hash` from what Shopify sends now; a later edit keeps the first base; a base the client sends is ignored;
  - the gallery is one field, its base the whole Shopify gallery (`product_images` ids are synthesized, `lib/schema.sql:28`).
- `resolveShopifyChange({id, revision, field, choice})`: `keep` re-bases on Shopify's current value, `shopify` sets the field to null. Draft only, through `replaceDraft` (a checkpoint first), so the "Changed in Shopify" notice clears at once.
- **Title gate at save:** a save (create, save, restore) that sets a NEW `title`, `display_type` or `display_vendor` while `titleGate()` is blocked is 409 `TITLE_NOT_EDITABLE` with the gate's words. A value the draft already held, and clearing one, are never refused.
- `openEntityContent({type, entityId})` finds the row by `(type, entity_id)` (a phase-3 random-id row too), creates one (two tabs at once: the loser reads the winner's), or brings an archived row back while the entity is in `products`/`collections` (the one `archived_at = NULL` statement in the code, guarded by `EXISTS`). It answers `inShopify` and `shopify` (what Shopify sends now, computed live), `titleEditable`, `titleBlockedReason` and `shopifyAdminUrl`.
- Deleting a product never archives its document; "no longer in Shopify" is read live.
- `discardOrphanEdits({id, revision})`: one batch `[documentsGuardStatement, orphan guard (aborts while the product is in products), DELETE the live row WHERE NOT EXISTS the product, the search_outbox 'discard' row (only when a row went; handle null), a checkpoint of the old draft, the draft reset to an empty body with nothing published, its abort, a 'discard' publish_log row]`. A product brought back by a webhook between the check and the batch keeps everything (409 `PRODUCT_IN_SHOPIFY`). A mode change is answered in mode words. Test seam `discardHooks.beforeBatch`.
- **Cart ruling (OQ-6 default):** `refuseCart` and `CART_NOT_AVAILABLE` are gone; `doc_cart` saves with storefront.write and publishes with storefront.publish.
- Archive is still refused while live; for product and collection content the words point to Use Shopify's and publish.

### (3) Title gate (`lib/catalog/title-gate.js`, new)

`titleGate()` is blocked when (a) `typesenseConfigured() && productsCollection()` for the current tenant (so `_default`, which uses the env collection, counts), or (b) `TITLE_READERS_READY` is false (it is). `setTitleGateForTest(state|null)` is the test seam. The belt in publish: while blocked, a product whose draft name/display field differs from its live version refuses the whole set, 409 `TITLE_NOT_PUBLISHABLE`, "The new name for “<Shopify name>” can't go live yet, …", with `documents: [{id, label}]` (today's set rule, a recorded deviation from PUB-03). It compares with the live version so a name that is already live never traps the founder's later edits. P4-SEARCH removes (a) and flips (b).

### (4) Live rows, publish and Undo (`live-rows.js` new, `build.js`, `commit.js`, `publish.js`, `undo.js`)

- `liveRowFor(type, body, {documentId, versionId, publishedAt, entityId?})` (CUTOVER uses the same function): modules are `pdpExtrasFromBody`'s `[{title, html}]`, in body order, plus an `image_with_text` module as its image, text and caption (sanitized), so a kept old module still shows; `gallery` `[{url, alt}]` with the first as `featured_image` and its alt; `description` plain text from `description_html`; `pairs` ids and `pairs_title`; `template` to `layout_template`; `keep_variant_images` 1/0/null; seo fields null.
- **A set of only these types** (`makeLiveRowBuild`, `runLiveRowBuild`, `commitLiveRowBatch`): one batch `[documentsGuardStatement, versions ON CONFLICT(id) DO NOTHING, per document UPDATE guarded on revision AND published_version_id AND its own version rows + abort, live-row upsert or delete, publish_log rows (release_id null), document_refs, one search_outbox row per product]`, no release row and no pointer move. The answer has `release: null`. Ids are minted once per attempt: a lib/db.js resend after a lost answer aborts on the guarded UPDATE and is found as ours by its log rows (proved on sqld). A closed store needs no home page for such a set.
- **A mixed set:** `makeBuild` gives these items a version, log row, pointer fields (guarded on `published_version_id`, `expectPublished`) and `liveRows`/`outbox`, but no list entry; `commitPublishBatch` appends their statements guarded on the pointer being `:new`, and leaves their versions out of `liveListHoldsMine`.
- **`documentsGuardStatement` in every publish-shaped batch:** `makeBuild` sets `build.guard = "documents"`; `commitPublishBatch` then puts the guard first and, after a failed batch, runs `classifyAbort`: a mode change is `{failed, code: "MODE"}`, which `runGuardedBuild`/`runLiveRowBuild` turn into `modeRefusal`: 409 `STORE_MIGRATING` "Your store is being updated. Try again in a few minutes." or 409 `STORE_USES_LEGACY` "This store still uses the current Storefront editor, so nothing was published here. …", never "someone else published". Hand-made builds without `guard` (the P2-ATOM suites) are unchanged.
- **Undo:** product, collection and cart content can be undone. Undo of a first publish deletes the live row (`plan.mode = "remove"`; "“X” shows what Shopify sends again. Your edits are still in the editor."). Undo and undoSet of only these types use the live-row batch; mixed ones ride the release batch. Each product gets a `search_outbox` 'undo' row. Recently published describes their rows with `describeChanges` and offers Undo.
- **Unpublish** (orchestrator ruling): 422 `CANNOT_TAKE_OFFLINE_CONTENT`, "Product content can't be taken offline. To go back to what Shopify sends, choose Use Shopify's on each field and publish." No live row deleted, no outbox row (also on a dry run).
- `search_outbox` rows are only ever written inside the batch that changes the live row; a collection never gets one; nothing drains them yet (P4-SEARCH).

### (5) Changes, reader, API, export

- `lib/documents/changes.js` `describeChanges(type, from, to)`: field by field ("Name: your own instead of Shopify's", "Name: back to Shopify's", "Badge changed"), a re-base alone gives no row, modules as sections. `listChanges` uses it for these types.
- `documents-reader.js`: `NOT_OVERLAID` now also lists `collection_content` (OVL07 does per-page draft preview).
- `documents-api.js`: `openEntityContent` (storefront.write, no revision), `resolveShopifyChange` (storefront.write, revision), `discardOrphanEdits` (storefront.publish, revision). A rows-only publish is audited by its set. Shapes in `documents-api-shapes.js` (`since: "p4"`), including the new refusals.
- `lib/admin/shopify-admin-url.js` (new): `shopifyAdminBase()` moved verbatim from `app/api/admin/catalog/route.js:25-29` (the route is not edited; ED05 switches it), plus `shopifyAdminUrl({product}|{collection})`.
- `lib/platform/export.js`: the tenant export now carries `product_content_live`, `collection_content_live` and `search_outbox` (null on a store before 0024). `cms_migration_runs` and `content_live_reversed` stay out: the format carries the store's content, catalog and its own state tables (`cms_state`, `sync_runs`), not operator tooling records.

## Known limits

- **Two test files outside the plan's list were changed**, both forced by the cart ruling and the deterministic ids, no assertion weakened beyond the ruling: `scripts/test-documents.js` (product content's id is now `doc_prod_…`; the cart case now proves create, save and restore work instead of the old 409) and `scripts/test-doc-changes.js` (keepAsEarlierDraft on the cart now keeps a version instead of 409). The orchestrator should add them to this item's files.
- **The editor's inspector** (`components/admin/editor/fields/inspectorModel.js`, not this item's) has no controls for `overlay`, `gallery`, `pairs`, `product_ids`, and its `WAITS.product_content` text still says publishing waits for OVL-02. P4-EDITOR owns both. `offeredKinds()` and `PRODUCT_MODULE_KINDS` are exported for its add-section list.
- **No shopper reads the live rows yet** (EFFECTIVE, OVL07). The sync acceptance case uses spec 8.9's COALESCE join directly, as `effective.js` is built in parallel.
- **The title belt compares with the live version**, not "any name in the draft": a name already live before the gate closed does not block later publishes of that product. Search already serves Shopify's name for it either way.
- **A rows-only set skips FIRST_PUBLISH_NEEDS_HOME** on a closed store: product and collection content open nothing.
- **`resolveShopifyChange` on a field that already follows Shopify** answers 200 with the document unchanged (idempotent for two tabs).
- **`pairs` and `pinned` give no reference rows** (`document_refs`, publish checks): they are ids, and the checks read products by handle. A pair or pin of a deleted product is not warned about at publish.
- **`discardOrphanEdits` writes its outbox row only when a live row existed.** A product that only had a draft affects no search entry.
- **`liveRowFor` maps an `image_with_text` module** to an accordion (image, text, caption); the product page shows it as a module, not as the side-by-side section it is on content pages.
- **Undo of a rows-only set reads the live pointer** (as Undo always did), so a documents store whose pointer is still a version 1 to 3 release is refused with the Releases words.
- **`openEntityContent` is a write** (it can create or un-archive), so it needs storefront.write and documents mode; a staff member can open product content, an owner or admin is needed to publish or discard.

From review:

- The 'same as Shopify stores null' rule (OVL-04) compares the founder's value with Shopify's raw HTML before it is cleaned. The stored value is the cleaned one. lib/documents/service.js normalizeOverlays calls sameValue(f, x.value, rawValueOf(f, raw)) before prepare() runs the cleaner. I tested this: a Shopify description of <p style="color:red">Soft <span>cotton</span></p>, saved back as its cleaned form <p>Soft <span>cotton</span></p>, was stored as the founder's own copy with a base. It did not become null. Real Shopify descriptions often carry markup the cleaner changes, and a rich-text editor will send the cleaned or normalized form. So the founder can silently stop following Shopify, which is exactly what the rule exists to prevent. The acceptance test only proves the literal byte-equal case. Suggested fix: also compare against the cleaned Shopify value (sanitize(raw.description_html)), or record that P4-EDITOR must send null for a description the founder did not touch.
- A rich_text (or text) module without a title saves and publishes with no warning, and it is missing from product_content_live.modules. pdpExtrasFromBody keeps only entries where title && html. I tested this: a rich_text module with title '' and html '<p>Care: wash cold.</p>' gave save issues [], publish issues [], and live modules null. The founder will see 'published' while shoppers never see that module (once P4-EFFECTIVE reads the live rows). Suggest a publish rule for product modules (a module needs a title), or a warning naming the module.
- Undo and undoSet skip the title belt. I tested this with the test seam: a name was published while the gate was open, then a reset to Shopify's name was published, then the gate was closed. Undo of the second publish put 'Secret name' live in product_content_live.title and back into the draft. Today this can't happen, because TITLE_READERS_READY is false and P4-SEARCH opens the gate for good. Still, it is a gap in 'publish re-checks it as a belt' and should be recorded as a Known limit, or the belt should be applied in undo.js.
- A product that is no longer in Shopify can still be published. I tested this: it writes a product_content_live row for the missing product and a search_outbox 'publish' row with handle null. That is harmless today. P4-SEARCH should expect a 'publish' row with a null handle, or publish should refuse an orphan with plain words pointing to discardOrphanEdits.
- Mutations that no test caught. (a) In lib/documents/publish.js rowMoved, I removed the publishedVersionId check (when another founder publishes the same product mid-way). All 26 cases still passed. No test covers two people publishing the same product at once in a products-only set. With the check removed, the answer would become 503 PUBLISH_BUSY after the retries, instead of 409 naming the other person. (b) In discardOrphanEdits, I changed the outbox insert's 'WHERE changes() > 0' to 'WHERE 1'. Still green, so 'no outbox row when no live row existed' is not tested. Mutations I made that the tests did catch: handle replaced with null in outboxStatement; the published_version_id guard removed in commitLiveRowBatch (caught only by the sqld resend case); the reconcile forced to ok in commitLiveRowBatch; the Undo outbox reason changed to 'publish'; search_outbox dropped from the export (test-tenant-ops failed); the 'unchanged name' allowance removed from gateNewNames; the orphan guard removed from discardOrphanEdits; expectPublished dropped in rowItem.
- The service still accepts a NEW image_with_text module sent straight through the API. It only gets the MODULE_NOT_OFFERED warning. Its image is not checked against the OVL-13 allowlist. I tested this: an image from https://evil.example.com/a.png published into live modules as an <img>. The gallery allowlist does not reach modules. This is within the brief ('not offered' is for the editor to enforce), but worth recording: P4-EDITOR uses offeredKinds, and the allowlist covers only the gallery.
- Caps that are lower than Shopify's own limits. The gallery allows at most 30 images, while a Shopify product can have up to 250.
